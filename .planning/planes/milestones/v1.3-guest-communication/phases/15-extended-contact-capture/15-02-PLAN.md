- **Email coupling to Prisma types**: research §Anti-Patterns. EmailService must NOT depend on Prisma `Guest`. Extend `BookingConfirmationParams` with 4 OPTIONAL scalar fields — never pass the full entity.

          private escapeHtml(str: string): string {
